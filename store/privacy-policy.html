Privacy Policy for PayPing Lite

Effective Date: October 26, 2026 • Application ID: com.paypingmy.app

The Core Principle

PayPing Lite is an on-device utility. We operate zero cloud servers, require no account or login, show no advertisements, and include zero third-party tracking or analytics SDKs. Your notification content, customer names, and sales totals stay strictly on your phone.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal Information: We do not collect or request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Network Traffic: PayPing Lite makes zero network requests across its entire lifecycle. It does not communicate with any servers.
  • Tracking & Analytics: There are no third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase, Google Analytics, Sentry, or Facebook SDK).
  • Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect device advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA).

2. How Your Notifications Are Handled

To announce payment notifications aloud and calculate daily totals, PayPing Lite uses your device's native notification access systems:

  • User-Controlled Filtering: PayPing Lite only reads alerts from the specific banking and e-wallet applications that you explicitly choose in the app.
  • Local In-Memory Parsing: Notification text is processed locally in memory using regular expression rules (parsers) to extract the amount and customer name.
  • On-Device SQLite Storage: Parsed results and raw notification payloads are stored solely in a local database file (payping.db) inside the application's private sandbox.
  • Text-to-Speech Output: Announcements are generated locally using your device's native speech engine (such as Google Speech Services on Android or Siri Voice on iOS). No voice data leaves your phone.

3. How to Delete Your Data

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over its retention:

  • In-App Deletion: Delete a single payment from its detail screen, or go to Settings → Data & Privacy and tap Delete Transaction History Only or Reset Entire App Database to wipe your local data immediately.
  • System Clear / Uninstall: You can clear app storage in your Android settings (Settings → Apps → PayPing Lite → Clear Storage), or simply uninstall the application. Uninstalling permanently and irreversibly deletes the local database.
  • CSV Export: You can export your transaction history to a CSV file at any time via the standard system share sheet.
